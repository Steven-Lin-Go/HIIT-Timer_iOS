常見問題

手機轉靜音了，為什麼還是有提示音？

這是刻意的。訓練中通常看不到螢幕，若靜音就沒有提示，會錯過區間切換。 不需要提示音時，請到「設定 → 音效」關閉。

語音倒數沒有聲音

請確認「設定 → 語音倒數」已開啟。語音使用 iOS 內建的朗讀功能，並依 App 的語言設定發音； 若系統從未下載過該語言的語音，第一次可能沒有聲音，到 iOS 的「設定 → 輔助使用 → 朗讀內容」 下載語音後即可正常。

訓練紀錄會同步到我的其他裝置嗎？

不會。所有資料只存在這台裝置上，App 不連線任何伺服器。換機或重裝 App 時紀錄不會轉移。

快轉跳過的回合會算進統計嗎？

不會。統計只計入計時器實際跑過的回合，所以「完成回合」與「完成率」反映的是你真正做完的量。

可以用自己的照片當背景嗎？

可以。「設定 → 底圖 → 選擇」挑一張照片，並用「顯示強度」調整明顯程度。 照片上永遠會保留一層遮罩，確保倒數數字清楚可讀。圖片只複製到 App 內部，不會外傳。

如何刪除我的資料？

「設定 → 清除所有訓練紀錄」可刪除全部歷史紀錄。將 App 從裝置移除，則會一併刪除課表、 紀錄、設定與底圖。

支援 Apple Watch 嗎？

目前沒有 Apple Watch App。

支援哪些語言？

正體中文與英文，可在「設定 → 語言」隨時切換。

Frequently asked

Why does it still beep when my phone is on silent?

That is deliberate. You are usually not looking at the screen mid-workout, so a silenced app would leave you with no cue at all. Turn it off under Settings → Sound if you prefer.

The countdown voice is silent

Check that Settings → Countdown Voice is on. The voice uses the iOS speech engine in the app's chosen language; if your device has never downloaded that voice, the first attempt can be silent. Download it under iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content.

Does my history sync to my other devices?

No. Everything stays on this device and the app contacts no server. History does not transfer when you change phone or reinstall.

Do rounds I skip past count in the statistics?

No. Only rounds the clock actually ran through are counted, so rounds completed and completion rate reflect what you really did.

Can I use my own photo as the background?

Yes. Settings → Backdrop → Choose, then set how strongly it shows with Visibility. A wash is always kept over the image so the countdown stays readable. The picture is only copied inside the app and never leaves your device.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Clear All Workout History removes every record. Deleting the app removes workouts, history, settings and the backdrop with it.

Is there an Apple Watch app?

Not at present.

Which languages are supported?

Traditional Chinese and English, switchable at any time under Settings → Language.
